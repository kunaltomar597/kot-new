# Usability test scripts (NFR-U06)

Run in the pilot with at least 5 people per role. Record each run in the table at the end.
The tester says nothing about how to do the task. Start the clock at the first tap; stop when the
task is done. Note every hesitation, wrong tap and question.

## Waiter: proficient in 30 minutes or less

Setup: a paired waiter phone, three tables open on the floor, a published menu.
Training: 10 minutes of the waiter manual only. Then the tasks, in order:

1. Sign in with the PIN. 2. Open a table for 4 guests. 3. Add three dishes, one with a modifier
   and a note; send the KOT. 4. Reorder one known dish and send it (5 taps or fewer). 5. Approve a
   pending diner order (2 taps or fewer). 6. Move the table. 7. Acknowledge a pager call. 8. Mark a
   service request resolved.

Pass: all eight done unaided inside 30 minutes in total, including training.

## Diner: first order in 2 minutes or less

Setup: a table tablet in kiosk mode and the QR menu on the tester's own phone (two runs).
Task: "Order a main course and a drink for the table." No help given.
Pass: the order is placed inside 2 minutes without asking for help. Also note whether calling for
water took one tap.

## Cashier: split bill in 1 minute or less

Setup: a table with six items and three guests.
Task: split the bill three ways and settle each share (cash, UPI, card).
Pass: the last share is settled inside 1 minute from tapping Bill.
