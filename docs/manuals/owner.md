# Owner's manual

You can do everything a manager can, plus the things that put the business at risk if misused.
Those ask for your password and a code from your authenticator app.

## Your own sign-in

Sign in on the dashboard with your password and the 6-digit code. Keep the authenticator on your
own phone. The Security page shows recent sign-ins, locked people and unusual attempts.

## What only you can do

- Change tax groups, GSTIN and invoice series (Restaurant page).
- Restore a backup, archive or purge old years, export all data (System page, Data).
- Manage the licence (Licence page).
- Create or remove managers (Staff page).

## Every day

Overview shows today's sales, open tables and alerts. Reports shows sales by day, dish, waiter
and payment type; the day-end report is the Z-report. Audit shows who did what, with who
approved it and why. Look at alerts first (late dishes, failed backup, low pager battery).

## Money and records

Bills and invoices are never deleted. A mistake is corrected by a void or an edit that records
who approved it and why. Invoice numbers have no gaps. Your accountant can take reports as CSV.

## Backups and the recovery key

The System page shows the last backup. Keep the **recovery key** in two safe places that are not
the restaurant PC. Without it a backup cannot be opened and the vendor cannot recover it.

## Updates and licence

Updates install overnight by themselves. The Licence page shows the expiry; renew before it.

## If something goes wrong

Ask the vendor's support. Do not share your password or recovery key with anyone, including
the vendor. For a stolen phone or a suspected misuse, tell support at once: the device can be
revoked within a minute from the Devices page.
