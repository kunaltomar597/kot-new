# Manager's manual

You run the floor and the day. Tasks below are in the dashboard (open from the main menu).

## Start of day

Overview: check alerts, the printers and that waiters have signed in. Staff page: today's
sections (which waiter has which tables). Floor page: tables and tablet locations.

## Menu

Menu page: add or change dishes, prices, modifiers, combos and photos; mark a dish out of stock
(the kitchen can too). Prices include or exclude tax as the restaurant is set up. To bring in a
whole menu use the import: check the file first, fix the report, then import.

## Staff and devices

Staff page: add a person, set their role and PIN, deactivate someone who leaves (people are
never deleted, so their past work stays on the record). Unlock someone who forgot the PIN.
Devices page: pair a phone or tablet by scanning its code, see battery and last contact, revoke a
lost device. Pagers page: issue and collect wrist pagers.

## During service

Orders page: the live feed with late dishes highlighted. Approve changes above a waiter's
limit by entering your PIN on their device: a void after preparation started, a discount above
the cashier's limit, a bill edit after printing. The reason is recorded. Move or merge tables.

## Ending the day

Close open tables and shifts, count the cash, then run the day-end. The day-end report is
produced; the business day changes at 04:00 by default. Check Reports for the day.

## Help

Every page has a Help button. Problems that need support: see the Owner's manual.
