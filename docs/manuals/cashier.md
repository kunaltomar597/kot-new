# Cashier's manual

You prepare bills, take payments and handle the till. You use the POS screen.

## Start your shift

Sign in with your PIN, open your shift and enter the opening cash.

## Taking an order at the counter

Pick the table or counter, add items, choose variants and modifiers, send. The kitchen gets it at
once. The system works out prices and tax; you cannot type a price.

## Billing and paying

Open the table, request the bill, apply a discount if allowed (within your limit; above it needs
a manager's PIN), choose the payment type (cash, card, UPI, split or part-payments) and print.
The invoice number is given when the bill is printed and is never reused. To split a bill by
item or by amount use Split.

## Mistakes

A printed bill is not changed freely. Reprint is allowed. A change after printing, voiding an
invoice or a void after cooking began needs a manager or owner to enter their PIN and give a
reason. Do not hand-write over a printed bill.

## Cash

Record cash taken in or out of the till with a reason. At the end close your shift: count the
cash, enter it, and the system shows any difference for the manager.

## When the system is slow or down

Say so to the manager. Use paper slips and the manual bill book (support playbook), and give
the manager the slips to enter later.
