Each video is under five minutes, filmed on the real screens with the demo restaurant seed, in
English. Voice-over lines are in quotes. Business Owner approval is needed before filming.

## Script outline for each video

1. Say who it is for and what they will be able to do (10 seconds).
2. Do the task once at normal speed on screen, naming each tap.
3. Show the most common mistake and how to fix it.
4. Point to the Help button on that page and the manual (`docs/manuals/<role>.md`).

### Example: video 2, taking an order on the phone

"This video is for waiters. You will take an order, send it to the kitchen, and see what
happens if the Wi-Fi drops." Sign in with PIN. Tap table 5. Add a dish with a variant, add a
modifier and a note "no onion". Send. Show the KDS receiving it. Turn Wi-Fi off, add a dish,
send: show "waiting to send". Turn Wi-Fi on: it sends once. End: "Prices come from the system;
you cannot change them. Ask a manager for anything else."

## Checking a video

Every step on screen matches the current app; no real customer data; no PINs or passwords
visible; subtitles on. Re-film a video when the screen it shows changes.
